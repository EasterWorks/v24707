When the printer has been moved to its permanent position in your workspace, you'll want to re-run input shaper tuning to ensure the best possible print results. You can find how to do that here:
https://www.klipper3d.org/Measuring_Resonances.html#measuring-the-resonances

Aside from the Voron Discord and subreddit, Ellis' Print Tuning Guide is the go-to guide for tuning up printers and getting the best possible results:
https://ellis3dp.com/Print-Tuning-Guide/

The Vorondesigns documentation website is also generally helpful:
https://docs.vorondesign.com/
